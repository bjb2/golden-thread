# The Golden Thread

A transmigration wuxia interactive fiction. **Books One & Two — Acts I–III, complete.**

**Play: https://bjb2.github.io/golden-thread/**

You die at a bus stop and wake on a mountain strung like a loom — the only living soul who can see it. Knots, debts, tournaments, a furnace fed on souls like yours, the rivers and lakes, and the Court that franchises it all. Choices persist; progress saves in your browser.

The final book — *The Silkworm Court & The Pattern Sage* — is in production. Your save will resume where Book Two ends.
