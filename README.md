# The Golden Thread

A transmigration wuxia interactive fiction. **Book One — Acts I & II, complete.**

**Play: https://bjb2.github.io/golden-thread/**

You die at a bus stop and wake on a mountain strung like a loom — the only living soul who can see it. Knots, debts, tournaments, a furnace fed on souls like yours, and the space between worlds. Choices persist; progress saves in your browser.

Act III: *The Rivers and Lakes* — in production. Your save will resume where Book One ends.
